## Read straight through

Friday afternoon, every takeoff in America stopped. Here's what actually broke.

At 3:54 Eastern, the FAA halted every departure. Four hours, twenty-one
minutes on the ground.

This is only the third time ever. First 9/11. Then a 2023 outage.

Once you're cruising, regional centers guide you between airports. Each hands
your plane to the next, by voice and data.

The official explanation: old routers at those centers got overloaded with
data. Voice lines filled with static. Screens flickered.

So the FAA held every takeoff, by design. The 3,500 planes already flying
still got to land.

Was it a hack? No evidence so far. Officials blame old gear, but the root
cause is still open.

Friday alone: fifteen thousand flights, delayed or canceled. And the
cancellations kept coming Saturday.

One weak link grounds a nation. That's availability. Security isn't only
about hackers.
